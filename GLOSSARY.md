# Real Estate Property Assistant

An AI assistant that helps leasing and sales agents produce on-brand listing descriptions faster.

## Language

### Inventory

**Unit**:
A physical rentable or sellable space (apartment, house) in the portfolio.
_Avoid_: Property, apartment, object

**Listing**:
An offer of a Unit on the market, for rent or for sale, with a price and a status. A Unit may have many Listings over time.
_Avoid_: Ad, offer, property

**Deal Type**:
Whether a Listing is for rent or for sale.
_Avoid_: Listing type, transaction type

**Listing Description**:
The approved marketing text of a Listing.
_Avoid_: Copy, blurb, ad text

**Draft**:
An AI-generated candidate Listing Description that an Agent has not yet approved.
_Avoid_: Suggestion, generation, output

**Brand Voice Guide**:
Marketing's written rules for how Listing Descriptions must sound: tone, do's and don'ts, banned phrases, length limits.
_Avoid_: Style guide, tone of voice, brand rules

### People

**Agent**:
A leasing or sales employee who manages Listings.
_Avoid_: User, broker, realtor

**Prospect**:
A potential tenant or buyer interested in a Listing.
_Avoid_: Lead, customer, client
